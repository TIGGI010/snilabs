Add it to Gmail

  1. Press Copy signature on the one you want.
  2. In Gmail on a computer: Settings (gear) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, name it "Startline".
  3. Click in the box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick hello@startlinelab.com and choose "Startline" for new emails and for replies.
  5. Scroll down and press Save changes.
