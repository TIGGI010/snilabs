Questions.

Anything else, just ask

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain and every account are registered in your name, and on handover you get everything. Nothing is held back, whether or not you take a care plan.

How does payment work?

Half before work starts and half before launch. Larger projects are split into milestones, all agreed in the written scope.

What isn't included in the price?

Third-party costs such as the domain, hosting or a Wix plan, paid plugins and card-processing fees. Those are billed to you directly, so you can see exactly what you pay for.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take two to three weeks from the signed scope. The biggest variable is how quickly the words and photos arrive.

Can I make changes myself later?

Yes. Sites are set up so you can edit text and images yourself. A care plan covers the changes you would rather not do.

What does an AI chatbot cost to run?

R450 a month covers hosting and the AI itself at normal small-business volumes. Very busy chatbots are quoted on their use. WhatsApp charges its own small per-message fees, billed to your account directly.

Do you charge by the hour?

Projects are a fixed price. Small jobs outside a project or care plan are R550 an hour, always agreed before any work starts.

Do you work outside Johannesburg?

Yes, anywhere in South Africa. Calls happen on WhatsApp or video, and the preview link means you never need to be in the same room.
