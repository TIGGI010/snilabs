Questions.

Anything else, just ask

Are these prices final?

No. Every price on this page and in the estimator is indicative. The price you pay is the one in your signed, written quote, agreed after a short call.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain and every account are registered in your name, and on handover you get everything. Nothing is held back, whether or not you take a care plan.

How does payment work?

Up to R20,000: half before work starts and half before launch. Above that: 40% to start, 30% at the working build and 30% at launch.

What isn't included in the build price?

Third-party costs such as the domain, hosting, email plans and card fees. They are billed to you directly by each provider, in your name.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take 7 to 10 working days once the words and photos are in. Online stores take up to 14. The biggest variable is how quickly content arrives.

What does an AI chatbot cost to run?

R450 a month covers hosting and the AI itself at normal small-business volumes. Very busy chatbots are quoted on their use. WhatsApp charges its own per-message fees, billed to your account.

What happens to unused care plan time?

Change allowances reset each month and do not roll over. Work beyond the allowance is quoted first or charged at R450 an hour by agreement.

Do you work outside Johannesburg?

Yes, anywhere in South Africa. Calls happen on WhatsApp or video, and the preview link means you never need to be in the same room.
