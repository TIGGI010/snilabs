Why we built it.

Petrol is priced by zone, so the real saving is the station that costs least to reach. Diesel pump prices differ from station to station, and nobody publishes them. Fuelwise ranks stations by what a fill actually costs, lets drivers keep each other honest on price, and keeps the log a fleet manager needs.

What it does.

Cheapest fill

Stations ranked by litres times pump price, plus the fuel burnt getting there, plus driver time if you want it.

Your car on screen

Fuel type, tank size, gauge and consumption, adjusted for load, aircon, towing and city or highway driving.

All four products

93, 95, diesel 50ppm and 500ppm at every station, with a warning if a 50ppm car is sent to 500ppm.

Route planning

Real road distance and traffic, the best stop order, and a warning when the tank will not cover the route.

Shared price reports

Drivers report the pump price, others vote it right or wrong, and a slip photo counts as proof.

Fuel log

Litres, rand per litre, odometer, station and driver, with real litres per 100 km worked out for you.

Fleet and drivers

Several vehicles and drivers on one account, each car with its own profile.

Savings and reports

Monthly spend, average price paid, cost per km per car and alerts for unusual fuel use.

How prices work.

Google has stations and distances but no South African pump prices, so Fuelwise builds its own.

01

Petrol is regulated

Every station in a zone must charge the official 93 and 95 price, so Fuelwise shows it and flags any station reported above it.

02

Diesel is reported

Stations set their own diesel price. Drivers report it, and two confirmations or an undisputed slip make it verified.

03

Trust scores

Reporters gain trust when others confirm them and lose it when marked wrong. Disputed prices are never used.

04

Monthly reset

Reports reset at each first-Wednesday fuel price change and expire after 21 days. With no report, a labelled estimate is shown.

Privacy and safety.

  • Location not storedYour position is used to search for stations and is not kept.
  • Secure accountsPasswords are hashed and sessions use secure cookies. Slip photos are visible only to signed-in users.
  • POPIA readyA privacy notice before public sign-ups, and slips deleted on request.
