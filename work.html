Startline Engine.

Live in 4 products

The engine behind our training apps.

An in-house, research-based plan builder and training engine: nine connected models turn sessions, sleep, HRV and race dates into a periodised plan that adjusts as training happens.

  • Powers Startline, Her Wellness, Startline Hub and the Tigz Does Tri plan builder
  • Built on 62 published studies, with every threshold traced to its source
  • Rules and algorithms, not a chatbot: the same answers always give the same plan
  • Available to coaches, clubs, events and fitness brands
