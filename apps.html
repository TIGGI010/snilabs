    App questions.

    Something else? Just ask

    Will it be on both app stores?

    Android apps go on Google Play. iPhone apps and both stores are quoted on a confirmed scope, and every listing depends on Apple and Google approving the app.

    Who owns the app?

    You do. The code, the store listings and every account are in your name, and you get everything at handover.

    What do the app stores cost?

    Google Play charges a once-off $25 for a developer account. Apple charges $99 a year. Both are paid by you, in your name.

    Why start with a prototype?

    A clickable prototype costs a fraction of the app and shows whether people use it the way you expect, before the big spend.

    Can it grow later?

    Yes. Version one is built so features can be added in later stages without starting again.

    How does payment work on an app?

    Projects over R20,000 are split into three: 40% to start, 30% when you approve the working build, and 30% at launch.
