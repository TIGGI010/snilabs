Training and wellness for women

Her Wellness.

Cycle-aware training and wellness for every stage of a woman's life, from first period to after menopause, with sport or wellness at the centre, her choice.

Why we built it.

Plans that ignore the menstrual cycle, life stage and how she feels ask women to fit the plan. Her Wellness turns that around. It runs on the engine behind Startline, with softer wording, fewer graphs and a calmer design.

Inside the app.

Real screens from the current build, shown with sample data.

What it does.

Readiness each day

Sleep, energy, mood, stress, symptoms and optional HRV, judged against her own normal for that half of the cycle.

A cycle that leads

Logged period days come first, so the plan and predictions shift when a period is early or late.

Sport or wellness

Running, trail, triathlon, cycling, swimming, team sports or gym, or wellness goals with strength, Pilates, yoga and mobility.

Event countdowns

With a race date the plan moves through base, build and taper, like Startline.

Activity and GPS runs

Record a run on the phone with a live map, then see pace, heart rate and efficiency by cycle phase.

Programmes

Couch to 5K, Runner's strength, Pilates core, Bone builder, Knee-strong season, Postnatal return to run and Strong pregnancy.

Athlete hub

A nine-question self-check on energy, periods, bones, pelvic floor, iron, fuelling and sleep, with actions.

Food and journal

Hand-portion food logging that adds carbs on training days, plus a journal with prompts by phase.

Run safe and Explore

Daylight times, WhatsApp to an emergency contact, a safety timer, and clubs, studios and clinics nearby.

Every life stage.

The stage changes which sessions are allowed, what is logged, which health checks appear and which articles show.

01

Teens

Age-appropriate options, meal-pattern guidance instead of portion targets, and under-13s gated at sign-up.

02

Cycling and contraception

Phase-aware plans and an optional fertile-window estimate for adults, clearly labelled as not contraception.

03

Pregnancy and postpartum

Week cards, pregnancy-safe sessions, and a guided postnatal return to running.

04

Perimenopause and after

Bone and balance work, menopause-aware fuelling, and flags such as bleeding after menopause.

Privacy and safety.

  • Private by designIn this version everything stays on her phone, with a PIN lock and her own backup and restore.
  • Gentle health flagsChecks by age and stage, and flags from her own logs, such as no period by 15 or 90 days without one.
  • Evidence, not adviceBuilt on published research and ACOG and NICE guidance. General information, not medical advice.
