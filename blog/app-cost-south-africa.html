AppsHow much does an app cost in South Africa?

By Startline Labs · 30 October 2026 · 5 min read

A business app in South Africa usually costs between R18,000 and R110,000 to build, depending on where it runs, how many screens and features it has, and whether it needs custom design. Here is how the price comes together, with our own starting prices.

Where the app runs

  • Web app, from R18,000. Works in any browser and can be installed on a phone's home screen. Best for staff tools, portals and testing an idea.
  • Android app, from R35,000. Listed on Google Play under your name. Most South African phones run Android.
  • Android and iPhone, from R50,000. One app for both stores, quoted on a confirmed scope.
  • Web, Android and iPhone, from R65,000. The full set with one shared back end.

Features change the price most

Each feature is real work to design, build and test. Typical additions in our app planner:

  • Accounts and login: about R4,000
  • Card payments: about R6,000
  • Bookings calendar: about R5,000
  • Push notifications: about R3,000
  • Maps and location: about R4,500
  • Admin dashboard: about R6,500
  • Chat between users: about R7,000
  • An AI assistant: about R8,000

Design and size

A clean, proven design is included. Custom-designed screens add about R6,000, and custom design with a logo and brand about R9,500. The first 12 screens are included, and each extra screen adds about R900.

Running costs after launch

Hosting for a small app is usually a few hundred rand a month and grows with users. Google Play charges a once-off US$25 developer fee, and Apple charges US$99 a year. Both should be in your name.

How to keep the cost down

  • Start with a clickable prototype to test the idea before the big spend.
  • Build version one around the single job that matters most.
  • Start on the web or Android, then add iPhone once it works.
