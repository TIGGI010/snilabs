Why we built it.

Most training apps either record what you did or hand you a generic plan. Startline joins the two: a plan built around your race that adjusts to how you actually slept, felt and trained. It started as our own tool for an ultra-trail block, and it is now being tested by Comrades marathoners, Ironman triathletes and ultra-trail runners.

Inside the app.

Real screens from the beta, on our own training.

What it does.

Race countdown

Pick the race and the plan runs backwards from it, through base, build, taper and race week.

Morning check-in

Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, energy and soreness, turned into a readiness score for the day.

Strava sync

Runs, rides and swims arrive by themselves every two hours, with years of history imported.

Metres per heartbeat

Grade-adjusted speed against heart rate on easy runs, so fitness shows up before race day does.

Paces and predictor

VDOT training paces and a race predictor that updates as fitness changes.

Load and durability

Training load, hill score, heat and trail durability, for the long days that decide a race.

Fuel guide and food log

Race fuelling settings and a food log built on South African food data.

AI coach

A coach that knows your plan and recent training. Ask a question, log something, or ask "what if".

Reminders

Push notifications for the morning check-in and the Sunday weekly review.

Our algorithms and AI coaching.

New algorithms we built for Startline, now the backbone of our other training products.

01

Athlete ratings

Speed, climbing, endurance, aerobic fitness, recovery and durability, each rated from your own data and rolled into one overall score.

02

Metres per heartbeat

Grade-adjusted speed per heartbeat on easy runs tracks aerobic fitness without a test or a race.

03

Readiness with confidence

Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate against your own normal, with a confidence level based on how many signals agree.

04

Load, form and durability

Training load, form, hill score and trail durability show whether a block is building you or digging a hole.

05

Adaptive AI coaching

After every session the AI coach checks the plan and makes changes, and says why. Easy days stay easy, key days move when you need them to.

06

Race priorities

A, B and C races shape the block, so the taper goes where it matters and the rest become training.

Tested where it hurts.

Beta testers who race the long, hard events, because that is where a plan either holds or breaks.

Comrades marathoners

Around 90 km on the road. Long blocks, back-to-back long runs and a taper that has to be exactly right.

Ironman triathletes

Swim, bike and run in one plan, with brick sessions and load that adds up across three sports.

Ultra-trail runners

Climbing, descending and time on feet, where hill strength and durability decide the race.
